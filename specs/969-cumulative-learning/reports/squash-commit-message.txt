feat(969): cumulative learning — accumulate across syncs and funnels

Learning aggregates no longer overwrite on every sync; evidence
accumulates all-time and carries across funnel types.

The unit of evidence changes from the Meta ad row to the creative.
Meta gives each placement of one image its own ad id; production
shows 1008 rows resolving to 146 creatives, one image spanning 55
rows. Grouping is by imageHash with generation links propagated
across the group, groups sharing a generationId merged, and manual
attribution taking precedence over automatic.

Activation gates FR-034 / FR-034a / FR-037 keep 10 / 3 / 3 and now
count creatives rather than ad rows.

A per-account lease inside runSyncForAccount fences the entire
learning read-modify-write. Phase 970's lease is keyed per owner and
does not reach the Cloud Tasks worker, which is the path the nightly
sync uses. Operational writes commit before the lease is attempted,
so a refused acquisition leaves the action list current and lets
Cloud Tasks retry.

Adds one owner-visible string, in English and simple Fusha,
indicating when an angle's evidence spans more than one funnel.

Not in this change, stated so main's history does not claim it:

  - The per-day conversion accrual and the write-once efficiency
    figure (FR-002, FR-002a, FR-077 to FR-087). learning/index.ts
    carries both modules commented out; the DayAccrual and
    ContributionState types are defined and unused. No cost or
    conversion-efficiency signal reaches generation as a result:
    a contribution carries click-through, cost-per-thousand and the
    verdict mark, and only click-through is ranked on.
  - Funnel-type WEIGHTING (FR-030, FR-031, FR-032a). The per-funnel
    breakdown and the multi-funnel indication ship; the retrieval
    path does not weight toward same-funnel evidence.
  - The creative-counted retrieval path (FR-033, FR-034a's floor in
    getTopWinners, FR-035's activation latch). getTopWinners.ts is
    untouched and still selects per ad row, so past-winner selection
    is not yet creative-counted.

All three are specified and tasked, not implemented.
